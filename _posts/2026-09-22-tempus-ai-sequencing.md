# Why I Chose Tempus

When sequencing technology is discussed, it is easy to focus only
on the science.

DNA and RNA sequencing can reveal genetic information that may help
researchers understand disease and help clinicians characterize a
patient's condition.

However, sequencing also creates another valuable resource:

**data.**

Tempus provides an example of how a company can combine sequencing
with clinical records, artificial intelligence, and data analysis.

This makes the company particularly relevant to fields such as:

- Nursing
- Public health
- Biology
- Genomics
- Bioinformatics
- Health informatics
- Precision medicine
- Clinical research

Tempus describes its platform as connecting clinical, molecular,
and other healthcare data to support physicians, researchers,
pharmaceutical companies, and biotechnology companies.

# Who Founded Tempus?

Tempus was founded by **Eric Lefkofsky** in 2015.

The company was originally established under the name **Bioin, LLC**
in August 2015. It later became Tempus Health, Tempus Labs, and
ultimately Tempus AI.

Lefkofsky became interested in improving the use of data in cancer
care following his wife's cancer diagnosis.

The company's founding idea was not simply to create another genetic
testing laboratory.

Instead, Tempus was built around the idea that healthcare decisions
could be improved by combining:

**clinical information + molecular information + technology.**

This became the foundation for the company's precision-medicine
business model.

# When Was Tempus Founded?

Tempus was established in **August 2015**.

The company was originally named Bioin, LLC before changing its name
during its development into the Tempus business.

Tempus later completed its initial public offering in June 2024,
with its Class A shares beginning to trade on Nasdaq under the symbol
**TEM**.

The IPO itself was not the company's initial funding.

By September 2017, Tempus had raised approximately **$130 million**
in total funding.

By March 2018, Tempus announced an additional $80 million financing
round and stated that it had raised **$210 million to date**.
